Done before the new corporate site is built, so nothing has to be redone after launch.

Phase 2: Search and AI visibility

$10,000 per month

  • Technical SEO monitoring and fixes

Search and AI results typically build over 30 to 90 days after launch. No specific ranking or result is guaranteed.

LAL practice page pilot

$500 per install

We install RxSight’s approved LAL page on each pilot practice’s website as part of onboarding. 60 practices, 10 per region.

  • The approved LAL page, placed and formatted on the practice’s site
  • The surgeon listed as a certified LAL provider
  • A link to the surgeon’s RxSight find‑a‑doctor profile
  • Clean structure, mobile layout and structured data for the LAL content

Compliance

How the pilot stays clean

The pilot is product onboarding, not marketing for the practice. These rules hold for every install.

  • RxSight pays us directly. Practices pay nothing for the install.
  • LAL page only. No SEO, marketing or other work on the practice’s site.
  • Approved content only. On‑label, with fair balance. Practices cannot change the claims.
  • Never tied to volume. Not linked to past or future LAL procedures or purchases.
  • RxSight compliance picks the practices and documents how they were chosen.
  • Value reported per practice so RxSight can handle Open Payments reporting.

A practice that wants SEO or marketing for its own site contracts with Local AI Registry separately, at its own cost, outside this SOW. Final program structure is subject to approval by RxSight’s legal and compliance team.
