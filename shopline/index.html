How we turned it around

Answer what buyers search. Earn the top spots.

Most people searching for ecommerce help never type "SHOPLINE." We built pages that answer the questions they do ask, then pushed those pages up the rankings until Google and AI assistants started sending them to SHOPLINE.

1. Find the demand

Non-brand searches: people looking for a store builder, POS, live shopping or ad costs who don't know SHOPLINE yet.

2. Publish the best answer

Blog guides and commercial landing pages written to win that search, with commercial pages cleared by SHOPLINE's legal team.

3. Climb and compound

Rankings move up, more searches see SHOPLINE, and AI assistants begin citing the same pages.
