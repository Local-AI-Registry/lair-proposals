01 · What we heard on November 23

You told us what works. This plan is built on it.

Ads are the wrong fuel right now

At today's volume, the marketing budget sits around $5k to $10k a month. Paid search and Meta at that level can't buy enough clinics to matter.

Reps won't door knock

Asking reps to cold call or walk into clinics doesn't happen. Building them a lead app doesn't fix that.

The story already closes

When a doctor gets on the phone with you, you close most of them. The product, pricing and service are not the problem.

The gap is access

The fastest, cheapest path to doctors is through reps who already have trusted relationships with them. So we recruit those reps.

"We focus on the reps, not the doctors, because the reps already have the doctors."Joe Wooten, November 23 call
02 · How it works

One portal, from invite to first order

Invite only. Small team, high quality. No base salaries, no mass hiring, and no one spends time on a rep who can't bring a book.

STEP 1

Invite

We load a list of reps. The portal sends each one a text, email or postcard.

STEP 2

Apply

Reps apply in the portal and answer your qualifying questions.

STEP 3

Interview

Reps who pass book a time with Jackie straight from the portal.

STEP 4

Sell and refer

Hired reps get the full kit, work their doctors and invite other good reps.

03 · The admin side

A recruiting engine you control

The strongest reps usually aren't on job boards. They get recruited. The admin side turns a list of names into booked interviews.

Load a list

We upload reps from aesthetics, pharma and medical device companies, plus 1099 reps who already sell 503A, GLPs or peptides.

Send invites three ways

Each person gets a personal invite by text, email or a mailed postcard. All three go to the same application.

Your Indeed ad plugs in

Responses from your current ad go to the same application, so every rep is judged the same way.

Every message includes an opt out. Postcard postage is passed through at cost, and only after you approve a send.

04 · Apply

Your qualifying questions, built into the application

Reps who answer well get a link to book with Jackie. Reps who don't are kindly turned away, and nobody's time is spent.

1
Do you sell GLPs or peptides today?A yes means they already know the product and the buyer. No training needed.
2
What does your current book do each month?Dollar volume in the last 30 days, across the accounts they manage.
3
How many of your doctors buy GLPs, and how many would switch for better pricing and service?Good reps answer this with names and numbers, not "all of them."
4
What would you commit to bringing in during your first 60 days?This becomes the hiring bar, the same way you set salary talks today.

Booked with Jackie

Qualified reps pick a time on Jackie's calendar. She runs the interview, 1099 paperwork and onboarding.

A sneak peek, not the playbook

Applicants see a short preview of the brand and story. It's enough to want in, without giving away the model.

$0

base salary

Reps earn on what they bring in. A rep who wants a salary has to put a guaranteed number on the table first.

05 · The offer reps hear

More money, less work, a better story

  • 15% residual on every order from their doctors, for as long as they're with NuGen. The industry norm is 7% to 10%.
  • Up to 20% for reps who bring a large proven book, set case by case.
  • Two lines to sell. 503A for doctors who want a pharmacy, and the NuGen line for everyone else. More ways to say yes, more income from the same doctors.
  • A portal no one else gives them, so they look more professional than the supplier their doctor uses today.
06 · The rep side

Everything a rep needs, in one portal

The rep's job becomes one text: "Doc, look at this." Everything after that is already built.

Resource library

The Dr. Razia explainer videos, white papers, one-pagers and a talk track that answers the questions doctors ask.

Product catalog

Everything a rep can sell, with what to know about each. They can pull it up in front of a doctor on a phone or tablet.

Order links

A rep sends a doctor a link that works like an online store. The doctor orders directly, and the sale is credited to the rep.

Lead tracker

Reps add the doctors they're working. The portal sends follow-up texts and emails on a schedule.

Follow-ups they can edit

Reps start from approved templates and can change the wording to sound like themselves.

Refer a rep

Reps invite other good reps they know. Referrals go through the same application and screen.

Commission view

Reps see their doctors, orders and earnings, which keeps them motivated without calls to you or Jackie.

NuGen owns the accounts

Reps see their own accounts, not the whole book. When a rep leaves, the doctors and data stay.

Onboarding inside

New reps learn the story and the tools in the portal, so you and Jackie don't repeat it on every call.

A brand reps are proud to carry

When clinics compare suppliers with the same product list, the brand they trust wins. We rebuild the brand once and everything uses it: the portal, the order links, the videos and the paperwork.

Included

Brand refresh

Name direction, logo, colors, type and a voice guide. It's applied to the portal, order pages, one-pagers and white papers, with Dr. Razia featured as the face of the brand.

One time · $5,000

10 explainer videos featuring Dr. Razia

Illustrated, animated explainers on sourcing, quality, COAs, service and how to order. Dr. Razia appears as an illustrated character, so she never has to sit for filming. She approves each one before it goes out.

11 · Guardrails

Built to protect the company, not just grow it

Counsel reviews the copy

Every portal page, video script, talk track and template is approved by NuGen and its regulatory counsel before reps can use it.

Facts, not fear

Videos talk about sourcing, COAs, stability and service. We don't make claims about patient outcomes or dosing.

Reps stay on message

Reps can personalize follow-ups, but they start from approved templates, and every message keeps an opt out.
