    How the math works

    Plays: brand interest below 80% of the US average is "Educate first", above 115% is "Sell now", otherwise "Build both". Reps go to cities by a demand score (60% toxin searches, 25% brand searches, 15% practice count). Each play splits its city's demand budget differently.

    Reps needed equals the sales target divided by what one rep's book sells by the deadline. Influence is the drop in conversion if one step is removed; impact per dollar is influence divided by cost.
