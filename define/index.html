2

April to July

We did everything the old site allowed. Then it plateaued.

Links, listings, citations and page fixes nearly doubled Define's website authority in three months. But clicks would not climb past about 340 a week. The old site structure was the ceiling.

Authority went up. Clicks stayed flat. That gap is how we knew the site itself had to change.

Authority: Ahrefs Domain Rating, start of each month. Clicks: Google Search Console, UK web search, average clicks per week in each month.

Proof, part two: AI

AI isn't keywords. It's prompts, so that is what we track.

On Google, people type a few words and pick from ten links. With AI, they ask a full question and get one answer that names a few clinics. There is no position 4. Where you can help, Tessa

London is the gap. These eight things move it fastest.

Beaconsfield is strong on Google and in AI answers. London is not there yet: AI names Define in 7% of London answers, and the London searches are still being rebuilt. We are writing the London pages now. These are the pieces only your side can do.

Why London is behind, and where each fix stands

  1. Google mixed up Define Clinic and Define Dental

    In progress

    The two share a phone number and older joint branding, so Google treated them as one business. That is what suspended the London listing in August, when London calls fell from 37 to 4.

    Where it stands: Listing restored in September. Dental's own number, 01494 851657, is agreed and waiting to go live. Then we separate the two across every listing. Ask 6

  2. Two pages for every London treatment

    Waiting on you

    After the August rebuild, each of the eight London treatment pages existed at an old address and a new one, and Google split its trust between them. That is how dermal fillers London slipped from 11 to 24.

    Where it stands: The plan to fold each pair into one page was delivered October 20. It starts once the seven questions in it are answered. Ask 3

  3. The London pages are only now being written

    In progress

    Until November, Google had no strong London page to show, so the home page carried London searches and was found mostly for the clinic's own name.

    Where it stands: London clinic page live in November. Dermal filler and laser pages are next. Moxi and BBL wait on confirming they are offered in London. Asks 3 and 4

  4. Far fewer London reviews

    Not started

    London has 66 Google reviews. Beaconsfield has 182, and two clinics near the London one have 201 and 280. Reviews are a large part of how Google and AI choose which clinic to name.

    Where it stands: Needs a review ask after every London appointment. Only the clinic can do this. Ask 1

  5. Old London addresses redirected outside the plan

    Waiting on you

    About 60 old addresses were redirected in mid October by someone outside our team, including three London botox pages pointed the wrong way.

    Where it stands: We are logging every redirect in one place and will re-point the wrong ones once we know who made the changes. Ask 5
