Full Face Neurotoxin in Merrick, NY

Softer lines across the forehead, frown and eyes, planned as one face so your expression stays yours.

Quick answer

Vixsthetics offers full face neurotoxin in Merrick, NY with Daxxify, Jeuveau or Xeomin. Victoria Yunaev plans the forehead, frown lines and crow's feet together, chooses the product at your consultation, and performs every treatment herself.

What does full face neurotoxin mean?

The muscles of the upper face work as a team. When only one area is treated, the untreated muscles can work harder, and brows can sit differently than you expect. Full face neurotoxin means Victoria looks at the forehead, the frown area between the brows and the crow's feet together, then plans doses so the whole face stays balanced.

The goal is fewer lines when you move, not a face that cannot move. Patients describe the result they want in their own words, and the plan is built around keeping the expressions they like.

Daxxify, Jeuveau or Xeomin: which one is right for you?

All three are FDA approved botulinum toxin type A products for frown lines. They differ in formulation. Xeomin is purified to remove accessory proteins. Jeuveau was developed specifically for aesthetic use. Daxxify uses a peptide based formula without human albumin, and its manufacturer reports that results last longer for some patients.

Having three options means the product is chosen for your face and goals rather than one product for everyone. Read more in Daxxify vs Jeuveau vs Xeomin.

What to expect at your appointment

  • A conversation about what you want to soften and what you want to keep
  • A look at how your face moves when you frown, squint and raise your brows
  • Treatment with a very fine needle, usually in a few minutes
  • Aftercare instructions from Victoria before you leave

Most people return to their normal day right after. Results begin to appear over several days and settle in about two weeks.

How long does neurotoxin last?

For many people, results from neurotoxin last around three to four months. Daxxify may last longer for some patients, according to its manufacturer. How long it lasts for you depends on the product, your dose and how strong your muscles are, which is why your follow up plan is set at your visit.

Neurotoxin Questions

Will I look frozen?

The plan is built to soften lines while keeping natural movement. Doses are set for your face, and Victoria asks what expressions you want to keep.

How soon will I see results?

Results begin to show over several days and are usually settled by about two weeks.

Is there downtime?

Most people go back to their day right after treatment. Small bumps at injection points usually fade quickly. Victoria gives you aftercare instructions at your visit.

Can neurotoxin be used for a lip flip?

Yes. A lip flip uses a small dose of neurotoxin along the upper lip. It is an off label use of an FDA approved product, and your provider will discuss whether it is appropriate for you.
