Sculptra in Merrick, NY

Gradual support and structure that builds over time.

Quick answer

Sculptra is a biostimulator made of poly-L-lactic acid. Instead of adding instant volume, it helps your skin build its own collagen gradually over a series of treatments. Vixsthetics offers Sculptra in Merrick, NY with Victoria Yunaev, PA-C.

How Sculptra works

Sculptra is placed under the skin, where it encourages your body to make new collagen. Results build gradually over the following months, so the change looks natural and it is hard for anyone to point to a single moment it happened.

Who it suits

Sculptra is often chosen by people who notice overall volume loss rather than one hollow, or who want subtle structure without the immediate change of filler. Victoria will tell you whether Sculptra, Radiesse (+) or a hyaluronic acid filler fits your goal.

See how it compares in Sculptra vs Radiesse.

How long does Sculptra last?

Because it works through your own collagen, results can last a long time. The manufacturer reports results that can last up to two years. Your plan includes how many sessions you need and when to return.

Sculptra Questions

Will I see results right away?

Some fullness right after treatment is from the fluid it is mixed with and settles. The real result builds gradually over the following months.

How many Sculptra sessions do I need?

Most people need a series. Victoria sets the number at your consultation.

Is Sculptra the same as filler?

No. Filler adds volume directly. Sculptra helps your skin build collagen over time.
