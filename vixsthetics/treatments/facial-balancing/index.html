Facial Balancing in Merrick, NY

Chin, cheeks, jawline, temples and lips, planned together so each detail supports the whole.

Quick answer

Facial balancing is filler planned across several areas at once, such as the chin, cheeks, jawline, temples and lips, to improve proportion. At Vixsthetics in Merrick, NY, Victoria Yunaev maps your face first and uses the RHA Collection and the Restylane collection.

What is facial balancing?

Faces are judged as a whole. A weak chin can make a nose look larger. Flat cheeks can make the lower face look heavier. Facial balancing starts with the proportions you already have and adds support where it changes the whole picture, rather than treating one feature on its own.

Areas Victoria treats

  • Chin, for projection and a cleaner profile
  • Cheeks, for support and lift
  • Jawline, for definition
  • Temples, where hollowing can make a face look tired
  • Lips and nasolabial folds, to finish the plan

How the plan is made

Victoria looks at your face from the front and in profile, at rest and when you smile. She explains which areas would make the biggest difference and in what order. Many plans are done over more than one visit, so you can see each step.

Facial Balancing Questions

How much filler does facial balancing take?

It depends on your face and goals. Victoria explains the plan and the amount before treatment.

Is it done in one visit?

Sometimes. Many patients spread the plan across visits to see each change.

Which fillers are used?

Hyaluronic acid fillers from the RHA Collection and the Restylane collection. For structure over time, Sculptra and Radiesse (+) may also be part of a plan.
