Under Eye Treatments in Merrick, NY

Filler, PRP, PRF and PRF EZGel for hollows and a tired look.

Quick answer

Vixsthetics in Merrick, NY treats under eye hollows and a tired look with hyaluronic acid filler, PRP, PRF or PRF EZGel. The right option depends on why your under eyes look the way they do, so Victoria Yunaev, PA-C, decides with you at your consultation.

Why under eyes look tired

Dark circles have more than one cause. Some come from volume loss that creates a hollow and a shadow. Some come from thin skin or pigment. Some come from puffiness. The treatment that helps depends on which one you have, and for some people the honest answer is that injections are not the right tool.

Your options at Vixsthetics

  • Hyaluronic acid filler can soften a hollow by replacing volume.
  • PRP, platelet rich plasma, is prepared from a small sample of your own blood.
  • PRF, platelet rich fibrin, is also made from your own blood, prepared differently from PRP.
  • PRF EZGel turns PRF into a gel that can be placed where support is needed.

Compare them side by side in under eye filler vs PRP vs PRF.

What to expect

The under eye area is delicate. Expect a careful assessment first, and possible swelling or bruising for a few days after treatment. Victoria will explain the plan, the number of sessions and aftercare before you start.

Under Eye Questions

Is under eye filler right for everyone?

No. Filler can help a hollow, but it may not suit puffiness or some skin concerns. Your consultation decides.

What is the difference between PRP and PRF?

Both are made from your own blood. They are prepared differently and have different textures. Victoria will explain which suits your goals.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the treatment. Filler is often one visit, while PRP and PRF are usually planned as a series.
