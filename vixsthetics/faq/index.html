Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about treatments, booking and visiting Vixsthetics in Merrick, NY.

Booking and your first visit

Who performs treatments at Vixsthetics?

Victoria Yunaev performs every treatment. Dr. Peggy Bastien, board certified in Obstetrics and Gynecology, is the medical director and supervising physician.

Do I have to be treated at my first visit?

No. Every first visit starts with a conversation, with no pressure to treat that day.

How do I book?

Where can I see prices?

Treatments and prices are listed on the online booking page.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are available on the online booking page.

Neurotoxin

Which neurotoxins do you use?

Daxxify, Jeuveau and Xeomin. Victoria chooses the product for your face at your consultation.

How long does neurotoxin last?

For many people around three to four months. Daxxify may last longer for some patients, according to its manufacturer.

Is a lip flip an approved use?

A lip flip is an off label use of an FDA approved product. Your provider will discuss whether it is appropriate for you.

Fillers and biostimulators

Which fillers do you use?

The RHA Collection from Revance and the Restylane collection from Galderma, plus Sculptra and Radiesse (+) for support and structure.

Can filler be dissolved?

Hyaluronic acid fillers such as RHA and Restylane can be dissolved if needed. Sculptra and Radiesse cannot.

What is the difference between Sculptra and Radiesse?

Both help your skin build collagen. Sculptra works gradually over a series. Radiesse gives some support right away.
