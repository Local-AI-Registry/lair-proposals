Lip Flip vs Lip Filler: Which Is Right for You?

By Vixsthetics, Merrick, NY · November 25, 2026

Quick answer

A lip flip relaxes the muscle above the upper lip with a small dose of neurotoxin so more of the lip shows. It adds no volume. Lip filler adds volume and can reshape the lip with hyaluronic acid. Choose a flip for a subtle change to how the upper lip sits, and filler for fullness or shape.

How a lip flip works

A few tiny injections of neurotoxin along the upper lip border relax the muscle that pulls the lip inward. The lip rolls slightly outward, so more of it shows. It can help if your upper lip seems to disappear when you smile.

A lip flip is an off label use of an FDA approved product. Your provider will discuss whether it is appropriate for you.

How lip filler works

Lip filler places hyaluronic acid into the lips to add volume, define the border or improve symmetry. Products such as Restylane Kysse and the RHA Collection are designed for this. Because it is hyaluronic acid, it can be dissolved if needed.

Side by side

  • Adds volume: filler yes, flip no
  • Changes shape: filler yes, flip only how the upper lip sits
  • How long it lasts: a flip usually wears off within a few months, filler often lasts six months to a year
  • Recovery: both are quick, filler may bring a few days of swelling

Can you do both?

Yes. Some patients pair a flip with a small amount of filler. Your injector will tell you whether that makes sense for your lips.

Common questions

Which lasts longer, a lip flip or lip filler?

Lip filler usually lasts longer. A lip flip often wears off within a few months.

Will a lip flip make my lips look bigger?

It can make the upper lip look fuller because more of it shows, but it does not add volume.

This article is general education, not medical advice. Talk with your provider about your own situation.
