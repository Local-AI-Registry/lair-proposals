Under Eye Filler vs PRP vs PRF: What Is the Difference?

By Vixsthetics, Merrick, NY · November 25, 2026

Quick answer

Under eye filler uses hyaluronic acid to soften a hollow by replacing volume. PRP and PRF are made from your own blood and are used to support the skin, usually over a series. PRF EZGel turns PRF into a gel. Which one fits depends on why your under eyes look tired.

Start with the cause

Under eye darkness can come from a hollow that casts a shadow, thin skin, pigment or puffiness. Each cause responds differently, so the first step is an assessment, not a product.

Hyaluronic acid filler

Filler replaces lost volume in a hollow. It can make a visible difference in one visit, but it is not right for everyone, especially people prone to puffiness.

PRP and PRF

Both start with a small sample of your blood. PRP, platelet rich plasma, is spun to concentrate platelets. PRF, platelet rich fibrin, is prepared differently and forms a fibrin matrix. Both are usually done as a series and work gradually.

PRF EZGel

PRF EZGel heats and processes PRF into a gel that can be placed where support is needed. Ask your injector whether it suits your under eyes.

How to choose

Bring photos of what bothers you and describe it in your own words. At Vixsthetics, Victoria Yunaev reviews the cause with you and recommends the option that fits, including when the right answer is no treatment.

Common questions

Is PRP or PRF better for under eyes?

Neither is better for everyone. They are prepared differently, and your injector will explain which suits your goal.

Does under eye filler last?

It often lasts many months, but it varies by product and person.

This article is general education, not medical advice. Talk with your provider about your own situation.
