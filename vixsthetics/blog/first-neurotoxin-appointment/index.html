Your First Neurotoxin Appointment: What to Expect

By Vixsthetics, Merrick, NY · November 25, 2026

Quick answer

A first neurotoxin appointment starts with a conversation about what you want to soften and keep. Your injector watches how your face moves, explains the product and dose, then treats with a fine needle in a few minutes. Most people return to their day, and results settle in about two weeks.

Before your visit

Think about what bothers you and what you like about your expressions. If you have had treatment before, note what you liked and what you did not. Share your health history and any medications when you arrive.

The consultation

At Vixsthetics, every first visit starts with a conversation, with no pressure to treat that day. Victoria Yunaev asks you to frown, squint and raise your brows so she can see how your muscles work, then explains whether Daxxify, Jeuveau or Xeomin fits.

The treatment

The skin is cleaned and a fine needle is used to place small amounts of product. Most people describe it as quick pinches. The whole treatment usually takes a few minutes.

After your appointment

  • Small bumps at injection points usually fade quickly
  • Follow the aftercare instructions your injector gives you
  • Results start to show over several days
  • Your result is usually settled by about two weeks

Your follow up

Your first treatment teaches your injector how your face responds. That makes the next visit even more precise.

Common questions

Do I have to get treated at my first visit?

No. Your first visit starts with a conversation, and there is no pressure to treat that day.

Does neurotoxin hurt?

Most people describe quick pinches that are over in minutes.

This article is general education, not medical advice. Talk with your provider about your own situation.
