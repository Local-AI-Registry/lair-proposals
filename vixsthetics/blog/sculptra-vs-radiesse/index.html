Sculptra vs Radiesse: How Biostimulators Differ

By Vixsthetics, Merrick, NY · November 25, 2026

Quick answer

Sculptra and Radiesse are both biostimulators that help your skin make collagen. Sculptra (poly-L-lactic acid) works gradually over a series of sessions. Radiesse (calcium hydroxylapatite) gives some support right away and builds collagen over time. Neither can be dissolved like hyaluronic acid filler.

What a biostimulator is

A biostimulator encourages your body to produce its own collagen. Instead of only filling a space, it supports the structure of the skin over time.

Sculptra

Sculptra is poly-L-lactic acid. Results build gradually over the months after treatment, and most people need a series. The manufacturer reports results that can last up to two years.

Radiesse

Radiesse is calcium hydroxylapatite. It gives some visible support when placed and stimulates collagen afterward. Radiesse (+) contains lidocaine for comfort. Hyperdilute Radiesse is mixed with more fluid to support skin quality rather than shape.

Which one suits you?

  • Want a gradual change no one can pinpoint? Ask about Sculptra.
  • Want structure in a specific area, such as the jawline? Ask about Radiesse.
  • Want to improve skin quality? Ask about hyperdilute Radiesse.

Because neither product can be dissolved, an experienced injector and a careful plan matter.

Common questions

Is Sculptra or Radiesse more natural looking?

Both can look natural. Sculptra changes more gradually, which some patients prefer.

Can biostimulators be combined with filler?

Yes. Many plans combine them. Your injector will explain the order and timing.

This article is general education, not medical advice. Talk with your provider about your own situation.
