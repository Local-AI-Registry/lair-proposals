Med Spa Near Oceanside, NY

Oceanside is just west of Merrick along the South Shore. Vixsthetics gives Oceanside patients physician supervised injectables planned around their whole face.

Quick answer

Vixsthetics is a physician supervised medical aesthetics practice at 1149 Merrick Avenue, Suite 17, Merrick, NY 11566. It serves patients from Oceanside, in the Town of Hempstead, Nassau County. Victoria Yunaev performs every treatment, including full face neurotoxin, lip filler and facial balancing.
