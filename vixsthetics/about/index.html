About Vixsthetics

Meet Victoria Yunaev

Victoria founded Vixsthetics in Merrick to give patients across Long Island personalized care that enhances their natural beauty.

Quick answer

Vixsthetics is a medical aesthetics practice in Merrick, NY founded by Victoria Yunaev, who plans and performs every treatment. Dr. Peggy Bastien, board certified in Obstetrics and Gynecology, is the medical director. Medical services are provided by Bastien Medical PLLC.

Book With Victoria (opens in a new tab)

Victoria Yunaev, founder and injector

After three years in the Cardiac ICU, Victoria turned her focus to medical aesthetics, bringing clinical precision and care to every appointment. She founded Vixsthetics in Merrick to give patients one consistent provider: the person who plans your treatment is the person who performs it, every visit.

Her approach is natural, never frozen. Treatments are shaped through restraint, balance and natural movement, and every first visit starts with a conversation about what you want to change and what you would rather keep.

Dr. Peggy Bastien, medical director

Dr. Peggy Bastien is board certified in Obstetrics and Gynecology by ABOG, with continued training in aesthetic medicine. She is the medical director and supervising physician for Vixsthetics. Medical services are provided by Bastien Medical PLLC.

How we practice

  • One provider, start to finish.
  • Three neurotoxins, Daxxify, Jeuveau or Xeomin, chosen for you.
  • Fillers from two collections, RHA and Restylane.
  • Comfort comes first.
