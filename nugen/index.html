03 · What changed

The market moved to the safe lane.

Since August, the FDA has been sending warning letters to peptide sellers. On August 24 it sent five at once, including one to a Texas seller. The FDA said a “research use only” label does not protect a seller when the products are really meant for patients.

At the same time, the door opened on the other side. In October, HHS and the FDA sent a new policy for review that would let 503A pharmacies compound six popular peptides for individual patients, including BPC-157 and TB-500.

So clinics are nervous about where their peptides come from. They are looking for a supplier they can trust and defend. That is exactly what NuGen can be, if we tell the story.

The risky road is closing. The safe road is where clinics are headed.

07 · Focus

What we are not doing, and why.

Paid search ads

Clicks on terms like “peptide pharmacy” cost about $13 each, and “compounded peptides” about $10. At your current sales, ads would burn the budget fast. Your reps convert better.

Selling to patients (B2C)

Consumer marketing in this category is expensive and hard. We stay 100% B2B: clinics and pharmacies only.

Our ground rules. Everything we put in front of clinics is about NuGen’s 503A, prescription-based products sold through licensed channels. Research-only products and unapproved drugs stay out of all marketing. That keeps NuGen in the lane that is growing, and keeps both of us safe.

10 · Your $500k goal

Honest answer: not in year one.

You want $500k to $1M a month by the end of 2027. Today you’re near $50k and falling. Our expected path gets you to about $122k a month by November 2027. That’s roughly a quarter of the goal.

Year one is about right-sizing the ship: stop the bleeding, get back to your summer levels and build the tools, story and pipeline that can scale.

Getting to $500k a month would take a much bigger engine, likely several times today’s sales team, more states and bigger accounts. We map that out together at the 6-month review, once we know what’s working.
