**Key takeaways**

  * Fat transfer moves a small amount of your own fat to areas that have lost volume, so it works as a natural filler.
  * ALMI is a regenerative fat transfer approach done under local anesthesia.
  * Results develop gradually, and the full picture can take several months.



## What fat transfer is

Fat grafting takes fat from one area of the body and places it where the face has lost volume. Because it uses your own tissue, the American Society of Plastic Surgeons describes it as a natural, autologous filler.1

## How ALMI differs

ALMI harvests fat with local anesthesia and uses the regenerative activity of fat tissue for volume. I am proud to be one of the developers and pioneers of the procedure.2 Patients stay awake and avoid general anesthesia.

## Who it suits

Fat transfer can suit people with volume loss in the cheeks, temples or under the eyes, including after significant weight loss. If volume loss comes with sagging skin, other treatments may be needed as well.1

## When you see results

The treated areas look softer and fuller, but an accurate picture of your results can take several months.3

## FAQ

Is ALMI the same as filler?

It does a similar job, restoring volume, but uses your own fat instead of a manufactured product.

Does it hurt?

ALMI is done under local anesthesia, so the treated areas are numbed.
