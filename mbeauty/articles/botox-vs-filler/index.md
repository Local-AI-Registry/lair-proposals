# Botox vs. Filler: Which One Do You Need?

They do different jobs. Here is how to tell which line, fold or hollow calls for which.

**Key takeaways**

  * Botox and other neurotoxins relax muscles, so they soften lines that appear when you move your face.
  * Fillers add volume, so they help with hollows, folds and thinning lips.
  * Botox usually lasts three to four months. Fillers can last from months to a couple of years, depending on the product.
  * Many faces benefit from both, used together.



## How Botox works

Botox is a neuromodulator. It blocks the nerve signals that tell certain facial muscles to contract.1 Lines between the brows and crow's feet come from those repeated movements, so relaxing the muscle softens the line. Results start within days, build to their full effect over several weeks, and usually last three to four months.2

## How filler works

Fillers restore fullness where it has been lost: the cheeks, the folds from the nose to the mouth, under the eyes and the lips. Hyaluronic acid fillers such as Juvederm and Voluma are made of a substance found naturally in the skin. Depending on the product, results can last from a few months to a few years.3

## Which one do you need?

  * **Lines that show when you frown, squint or raise your brows:** usually Botox.
  * **Lines that are there even when your face is still:** often filler, sometimes both.
  * **Flat cheeks, hollow under-eyes or thin lips:** filler.
  * **A heavy or square jaw:** Botox for jaw slimming.



Filler is not the answer to every concern. When it is right for the patient and placed artistically, it can be powerful.4

## Choose an experienced injector

Overfilling and other complications can happen when fillers are injected by someone without the right training.1 Ask who will treat you, what their training is and what product they will use.

## FAQ

Can I get Botox and filler at the same visit?

Often, yes. Many plans use both, since they solve different problems.

Which lasts longer?

Filler usually lasts longer than Botox, but it depends on the product and the area.
