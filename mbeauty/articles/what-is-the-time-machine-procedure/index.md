## Why treat everything at once

Aging does not happen in one place. Sun damage changes the color and quality of the skin, collagen loss brings lines and sagging, the cheeks and under-eyes lose volume, and the muscles we use to frown and squint deepen the creases. Treating only one of those can leave the others looking out of place. That is why I created the Time Machine Procedure™: one plan that addresses every factor together.2

## What goes into a Time Machine plan

There is no single recipe. After looking at your skin, we combine treatments such as injectables for lines and volume, radiofrequency and laser energy for texture and firmness, light-based treatments for spots and redness, and regenerative options when they fit. The aim is total rejuvenation, more complete than the tightening of loose skin that comes from a face lift.

## FAQ

Is the Time Machine Procedure™ a face lift?

No. It is non-surgical. It treats skin quality, lines, volume and sagging together without a scalpel.

How long do results last?

It depends on your skin and your maintenance plan. Your consultation covers what to expect.
