Questions

Answers before you visit

What is the Time Machine Procedure™?

It is Dr. Tess's signature combination of the latest technologies and her expertise, built to treat sun damage, wrinkles, sagging, lost volume and overactive expression lines together. Every patient gets a personal plan, and no surgery is required.

Is my consultation free?

Consultations with our cosmetic consultants are free. A consultation with Dr. Tess is $250, and that fee is applied toward any purchase or service.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. 0% financing is available upon approval.

Where are your clinics?

We have three clinics in California: 421 North Rodeo Drive in Beverly Hills, 9888 Carroll Centre Road in San Diego and 875 Bowsprit Road in Chula Vista.

Who is Dr. Tess?

Dr. Tess Mauricio, MD, FAAD, is a board-certified dermatologist and a graduate of Stanford University School of Medicine. She founded M Beauty Clinic with her husband, Dr. James Lee, and is known worldwide as America's Favorite Dermatologist.

Can I buy Dr. Tess's skincare online?

Yes. Visit our Shop section for Dr. Tess's partner stores, including Platform Health, Xtresse, Hydrinity and AlumierMD.
