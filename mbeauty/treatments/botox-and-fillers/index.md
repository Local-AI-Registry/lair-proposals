## Botox and Fillers FAQ

How long does Botox last?

Results are temporary and typically last a few months, which is why most people schedule regular maintenance visits.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel a quick pinch. The clinic uses very fine needles, and numbing is available.

What are fillers made of?

Juvederm and Revanesse are made of hyaluronic acid, a substance that occurs naturally in the skin.

Is there downtime?

Most people return to normal activities right away. Small bruises or swelling can happen and usually fade within days.

Should I get Botox or filler?

They do different jobs. Botox relaxes lines caused by movement, while filler replaces lost volume. Read [Botox vs. filler: which one do you need?](/mbeauty/articles/botox-vs-filler/)
