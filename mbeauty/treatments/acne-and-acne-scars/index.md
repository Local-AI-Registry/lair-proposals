## Acne and Acne Scars FAQ

Can you treat acne and scars at the same time?

Often, yes. The plan usually calms active acne first, then focuses on marks and scars.

Which scar treatment is best for darker skin?

Options such as Micropen microneedling are often chosen for darker skin. Fraxel ReStore is best suited to lighter skin types.

Do I need to see a dermatologist for acne?

Persistent, painful or scarring acne is worth a visit, since prescription care can prevent new scars.
