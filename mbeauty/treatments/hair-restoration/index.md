## Hair restoration options

  * **Natural hair transplant.** An automated system using the follicular unit extraction (FUE) method.
  * **ALMI Hair Restoration.** Targeted placement of regenerative cells to support the hair cycle.
  * **Hairvolution (PRP).** Platelet-rich plasma from your own blood for hair regrowth.
  * **HydraFacial Keravive.** A scalp facial for scalp health and natural growth.
  * **REVIAN RED.** An LED light therapy cap for thinning hair, used at home.

## Hair Restoration FAQ

Do you treat women with hair loss?

Yes. The clinic treats men and women with slight, moderate or severe hair loss.

What is FUE?

Follicular unit extraction moves individual hair follicles to thinning areas, without a long linear scar.

Is PRP painful?

Numbing is used to keep scalp injections comfortable.
