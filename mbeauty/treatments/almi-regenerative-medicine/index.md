## What is ALMI?

ALMI harvests a small amount of your own fat with local anesthesia and places it where volume has been lost. The aesthetic trade publication _The Aesthetic Guide_ describes it as a regenerative aesthetics approach that uses the stem cell activity of fat for volumization, and notes that Dr. Tess helped pioneer it.1 Because it uses your own tissue, it can work as a natural filler.

## How it is used

  * **Face.** ALMI fat transfer to restore volume and refresh the skin.

## ALMI and Regenerative Medicine FAQ

Is ALMI surgery?

ALMI is done under local anesthesia, so you stay awake and avoid general anesthesia.

Where does the fat come from?

From your own body, usually an area with a little extra fat. The amount needed is small.

Who developed ALMI?

Dr. Tess Mauricio is one of the developers and pioneers of the ALMI procedure.

How long until I see results?

Results build gradually. Your consultation covers what to expect for your goals.
