## Why it is called the Time Machine

Dr. Tess named it after what patients kept telling her: they looked like they had traveled back in time to how they looked 5 to 10 years earlier. The goal is not a different face. It is your face, rested and renewed, without the changes that can come with surgery.

NBC's _California Live_ featured the procedure in 2019, calling the result a "Benjamin Button effect."1

## What it treats

Aging shows up in several ways at once, so the Time Machine treats them together instead of one at a time:

  * Sun damage, discoloration and skin quality
  * Wrinkles and fine lines
  * Sagging skin along the cheeks, jaw and neck
  * Lost volume in the cheeks and under the eyes
  * Overactive muscles that deepen expression lines



Patients see the change in their eyelids, under-eye area, cheeks, pores and the overall quality of their skin.

## How it works

Every plan starts with a consultation. Dr. Tess and her team look at your skin, your goals and your schedule, then build a combination of treatments for you.
