## Fraxel laser resurfacing

Fraxel uses thousands of microscopic laser columns to treat a fraction of the skin at a time, which triggers younger, smoother skin with less downtime than older ablative lasers. The clinic offers the mid-strength Fraxel treatment rather than the CO2-based Fraxel RePair.1

## Sofwave and radiofrequency microneedling

Sofwave is FDA cleared to stimulate new collagen for eyebrow lifting, lines under the chin and on the neck, and fine lines and wrinkles. PixeLift combines radiofrequency energy with microneedling to improve texture and tighten skin.2

## Laser and Skin Tightening FAQ

How much downtime is there?

It depends on the treatment. Many options have little to none, and your consultation matches the treatment to your schedule.

Does laser hair removal hurt?

Most people describe it as a rubber band snap. The clinic applies numbing cream and cools the skin during treatment.

How should I prepare for laser treatment?

Avoid sun exposure for at least two weeks before, and do not bleach, pluck or wax for two weeks before and after hair removal.

Which treatment is right for me?

It depends on your skin type, goals and downtime. A consultation with the team sets your plan.
