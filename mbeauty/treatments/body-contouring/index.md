## Tickle Lipo

Tickle Lipo, also called nutational infrasonic liposculpture, removes stubborn fat under local anesthesia, so you stay awake and avoid general anesthesia. Infrasonic vibration helps block pain, and the gentle technique means less bruising and swelling and a faster return to normal life than traditional liposuction.1

## Body Contouring FAQ

Is Tickle Lipo done under general anesthesia?

No. It uses local anesthesia, so you stay awake.

How soon can I get back to normal?

Most people return to normal life in days, not weeks.

Which areas can be treated?

Common areas include the abdomen, waist, hips, arms, neck, jawline, thighs and knees.
