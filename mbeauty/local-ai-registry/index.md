## How the AI Score works

Every business on the registry gets an AI Score from 0 to 100 for how findable and recommendable it is to AI assistants. It is built from eight weighted signals, and the heaviest is simply whether AI can retrieve and cite the business at all:

  * Retrieval grounding
  * Off-property attestation
  * AI visibility
  * Reviews and response
  * Backlink and citation authority
  * Google Business Profile
  * Speed and mobile
  * Content freshness and schema



The same method applies to every business, and nobody can pay to rank. Read [the full AI Score methodology](https://localairegistry.com/methodology), or the plain-language guide [What is an AI Score, and how is it calculated?](https://localairegistry.com/journal/what-is-an-ai-score)

## Why a dermatologist helps set the standard

In aesthetics, the stakes are higher than a bad meal. A recommendation sends someone to a person who will inject, laser or treat their skin. Training, board certification and a record of real results should carry the most weight, and a physician is best placed to say which signals actually reflect quality.
