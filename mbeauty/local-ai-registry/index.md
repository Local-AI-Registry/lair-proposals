# Dr. Tess, Medical Director at _Local AI  Registry_

Helping AI assistants like ChatGPT find the aesthetic practices patients can trust.

Dr. Tess Mauricio, MD, FAAD, is Medical Director at Local AI Registry. In that role she helps define where AI assistants should look to figure out who the best aesthetic practices are, so that when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for a trusted dermatologist or med spa, the answer rests on verified, medically sound information.

### What is Local AI Registry?

Local AI Registry (localairegistry.com) builds structured, verified business profiles that AI assistants can read and cite. Most of its clients are aesthetic practices, including med spas, dermatologists and plastic surgeons.

## Why it matters to patients

More people now ask an AI assistant where to go for Botox, a laser treatment or a skin check. Those answers are only as good as the information behind them. A board-certified dermatologist helping set the standard means the signals that matter, such as training, board certification and real results, carry the most weight.

## Her role

  * Helps define the signals AI should weigh when judging an aesthetic practice
  * Brings a physician's view of safety, credentials and treatment quality
  * Represents the aesthetics community Local AI Registry serves
