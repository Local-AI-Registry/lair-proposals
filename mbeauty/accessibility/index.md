# Accessibility

M Beauty Clinic by Dr. Tess

M Beauty Clinic by Dr. Tess wants this website to be usable by everyone. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

## What we do

  * Readable text with strong color contrast
  * Descriptive text for images
  * Keyboard-friendly navigation and forms

If anything on this site is hard to use, call 1 (833) 462-3288 or email info@mbeautyclinic.com and we will help you directly.
