# Privacy Policy

M Beauty Clinic by Dr. Tess

M Beauty Clinic by Dr. Tess respects your privacy. This page explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

## What we collect

When you request a consultation, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email, preferred clinic and the treatment you are interested in. We use them only to contact you about your request.

## Health information

Please do not send detailed medical information through this website. We will discuss your health privately during your consultation, and medical records are protected under applicable law, including HIPAA.

## Cookies and analytics

This website may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use it. We do not sell your personal information.
