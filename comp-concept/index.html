The rules

  • Source and book: 5% for team members, 15% for KOLs and partners who bring in the client and get the meeting booked.
  • Close: 10%, fixed, to whoever gets the contract signed.
  • Splits: if two people share a role, they split that role's percentage. Shares must add up to 100%.
  • One person, both roles: they earn both shares, up to 25%.
  • Paid on cash collected, monthly, for the agreed number of months.
