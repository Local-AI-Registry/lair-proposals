Case Study: Local AI Registry

Named by AI in 90 days,
or they work for free.

How absolute R relations turned an AI visibility guarantee into stories editors want to run and expert sources they keep calling.

The plan tied the guarantee to OpenAI's ChatGPT business launch, offered three story angles and three expert sources, and prioritized exclusives and followed links in outlets AI engines cite.

Local AI Registry

The client

Local AI Registry builds structured, verified business profiles that AI engines can read and cite. When someone asks ChatGPT for the best med spa nearby or a top plastic surgeon in their city, Local AI Registry's job is to make sure its clients are part of the answer.

Its headline offer is unusually bold for marketing services: named by AI within 90 days, or the team works for free until you are. Billing stops until the client sees proof, and every check comes with screenshots.

The challenge

A guarantee sells on a sales call. It does not, by itself, get a reporter's attention. We faced three problems:

  • It sounded like an ad. Editors do not run promotions, and a promise alone is not news.
  • The topic was technical. AI search, entity data and citations lose most readers in the first sentence.
  • The goal was unusual. Local AI Registry did not want beauty shoppers clicking through. It wanted authority: exclusives and followed links in publications AI engines trust, so its clients would be named in AI answers for plastic surgeons and med spas.

The strategy

1. Make the guarantee news

In November 2026, OpenAI opened business profiles in ChatGPT. Every local business suddenly wondered whether it needed one. We framed the story around the gap most coverage missed: claiming a profile does not mean ChatGPT will recommend you. The 90-day guarantee became the answer to that question, not a sales line.

2. Three angles for three desks

Consumer safety

How patients are choosing doctors and med spas through AI, and what to check before trusting the answer.

Practice owners

Trade advice: what owners should do now that ChatGPT lists businesses, and why a profile alone is not enough.

Founder feature

A former Google Search executive who opened his own med spa, got burned by agencies, and built the fix.

3. A bench of expert sources

Reporters on deadline need someone quotable today. We gave them three.

SLSteve LeeCEO

Former Google Search and Microsoft executive with 20+ years in digital. Owns a med spa.

APAneesh Patel, PharmDCo-founder & CGO

Aesthetics industry veteran of Allergan, Galderma and Evolus.

BDBridgette DeBrinoVP, Marketing & Sales

Speaks to how local businesses market themselves in the AI era.

4. Placements chosen for AI, not just applause

We agreed on a simple standard with the Local AI Registry team: exclusives first, followed links preferred, and outlets that AI assistants already cite for health, beauty and local recommendations. A plain mention with no link was not the win. Every pitch was checked against that rule before it went out.

5. A shared pitch board

Local AI Registry built a simple, plain-language idea board for our team, so we could pull a fresh angle the moment a relevant AI or search story broke.

“Colleen took something technical and made it a story editors understand. She knew which reporters cared, and she pushed for the links that actually help us get cited by AI.”
SLSteve Lee
CEO, Local AI Registry

The result

Local AI Registry now has what most tech companies never build: a news hook that renews itself every time AI search changes, three expert voices editors can call, and a placement standard that ties every story back to its real goal of being cited by AI.

  • A guarantee reframed from sales term to news story
  • Three ready-to-run angles for consumer, trade and founder coverage
  • Three named expert sources with approved bios
  • A link-first placement standard for every pitch

What other brands can learn

  1. Pair a promise with a news moment. A guarantee becomes a story when it answers a question readers are already asking.
  2. Give editors people, not products. Named experts with clear credentials get quoted. Logos do not.
  3. Pick placements AI will read. In the AI era, where a story runs and how it links matter as much as how many people see it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Local AI Registry 90-day guarantee?

Local AI Registry guarantees that a business will be named by AI within 90 days, or the team works for free until it is. The business chooses what it wants to be named for through its registry record, every check comes with screenshots in a monthly report, and free means billing stops until proof is shown. It applies to Local AI Registry's top-tier plan.

Can PR help a business show up in ChatGPT answers?

Yes. AI assistants build answers from sources they trust, and established publications are among the most cited. PR that places a brand and its experts in those outlets, with consistent facts and clear attribution, gives AI more reasons to name the brand.

Why do followed links matter for AI visibility?

A followed link tells search engines that a publication vouches for a website, which builds domain authority. Authority and clear mentions in trusted outlets both make it more likely that Google and AI assistants surface a brand. A mention with no link still helps people, but does less for search.

Who are Local AI Registry's expert sources?

Steve Lee, CEO and a former Google Search and Microsoft executive who also owns a med spa; Aneesh Patel, PharmD, Co-founder and Chief Growth Officer with aesthetics experience at Allergan, Galderma and Evolus; and Bridgette DeBrino, VP of Marketing and Sales.

How did absolute R relations pitch Local AI Registry?

We tied the guarantee to OpenAI's ChatGPT business launch, built three story angles for consumer, trade and founder desks, offered three expert sources, and prioritized exclusives and followed links in outlets AI engines cite.
