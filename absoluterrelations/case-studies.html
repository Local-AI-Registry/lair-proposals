Local AI Registry

Featured case study

Pitching a 90-day AI visibility guarantee to the press

Industry
AI visibility for local businesses
Services
Media relations, expert sourcing, AI-ready PR
Year
2026

The challenge

Local AI Registry helps med spas, plastic surgeons and other local businesses get named by ChatGPT and Google AI. Its boldest promise is a guarantee: named by AI within 90 days, or the team works for free until you are. A guarantee is a sales term, not a story, and the goal was authoritative coverage that AI engines read and cite.

What we did

  • Tied the guarantee to breaking news: OpenAI's ChatGPT business launch, and the gap that claiming a profile does not mean ChatGPT will recommend you
  • Built three angles for three desks: consumer safety, practice-owner trade advice, and a founder feature
  • Assembled a bench of three expert sources: CEO Steve Lee, Chief Growth Officer Aneesh Patel, PharmD, and VP of Marketing and Sales Bridgette DeBrino
  • Set a placement standard of exclusives and followed links in outlets AI engines cite

The outcome

Local AI Registry went from a product with a promise to a ready-to-run story kit with an expert bench editors can call on any AI and local search news cycle.

Mama Sol

An expert voice in Byrdie for a mineral sun care brand

Industry
Mineral sun care and skincare
Services
Expert sourcing, media relations, link review
Year
2026

The challenge

Mama Sol, a mineral sunscreen and skincare brand, wanted credible editorial coverage that also built authority for its own website.

What we did

  • Positioned the brand's medical consultant, Dr. Sheri Rowen, as an expert source for beauty editors
  • Secured her quote in Byrdie's story on Korean lash lifts, with her name linked to her bio on the Mama Sol site
  • Reviewed the live link with the client team, then gave the editor clear, copy-ready language to request a followed link

The outcome

A named expert quote in a leading beauty outlet, plus a repeatable playbook for requesting followed links on future expert placements.
