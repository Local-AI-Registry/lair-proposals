Services, answered

What is included in a beauty PR retainer?

A typical absolute R relations retainer includes a 360° PR plan, daily media outreach, long-lead and online pitching, product sampling, influencer seeding, monthly reporting and award submissions. TV, events and paid creator partnerships are scoped to your launch.

Do you only work with beauty brands?

Beauty is our core, including skincare, haircare, salon, sun care and body care. We also represent wellness, intimate care, supplements, better-for-you food, fragrance and luxury lifestyle brands, and we pitch trade, consumer and influencer channels.

What is AI-ready PR?

AI-ready PR is media relations planned so AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity can find and trust your brand. It favors outlets AI engines cite, followed links, named experts and consistent brand facts across every story.

Do you handle influencer gifting and paid partnerships?

Yes. We seed product to a network of more than 1,000 micro and macro creators and manage paid partnerships for major launches, including flatlays, stories, boomerangs and video.

Can you produce events outside New York?

Yes. We produce in-person events in New York and other markets, and virtual previews for national media. We manage guest lists, RSVPs, logistics and content capture through Absolute R Productions.
