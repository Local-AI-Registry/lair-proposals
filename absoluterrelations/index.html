Questions, answered

Beauty PR FAQ.

What does absolute R relations do?

absolute R relations is a New York City PR agency for beauty, wellness, lifestyle and luxury brands. We handle media relations, national TV, influencer seeding, events and activations, beauty award strategy and AI-ready PR that helps brands appear in ChatGPT and Google AI answers.

Who founded absolute R relations?

Colleen Mathis founded absolute R relations in 2015. She has more than 20 years in PR and marketing across beauty, lifestyle and luxury, has managed campaigns for Procter & Gamble brands, and has landed features in THE TODAY SHOW, Vogue, InStyle, W, People, Allure and Town & Country.

Which magazines have absolute R relations clients appeared in?

Client brands have been featured in Vogue, ELLE, Harper's BAZAAR, Allure, Marie Claire, InStyle, Glamour, People, W, Town & Country, Brides, NewBeauty, The New York Times, WWD, POPSUGAR, Refinery29, Bustle, NYLON, Byrdie, Forbes, Fashionista and THE TODAY SHOW, plus salon trade titles Modern Salon and American Salon.

Where is absolute R relations located?

Our office is at 1270 6th Avenue, Suite 711, New York, NY 10020, in Midtown Manhattan near Rockefeller Center. We work with brands across the United States and international brands entering the US market.

What kinds of brands do you work with?

Skincare, haircare and salon brands, body care, intimate care, sun care, wellness and supplements, better-for-you food, fragrance and luxury lifestyle products. Many clients sell at Sephora, Ulta Beauty, Target, Walmart, Amazon, CVS, Walgreens, QVC and HSN.

How long does beauty PR take to get results?

Online outlets can move in a few weeks. Monthly magazines plan three to six months ahead, so long-lead placements need early pitching. Most brands see the strongest results by planning PR around a launch date at least one season out.

Can PR help my brand show up in ChatGPT and AI search?

Yes. AI assistants lean on trusted publications when they answer questions like which sunscreen is best or which med spa to visit. Our AI-ready PR prioritizes outlets AI engines cite, followed links and clear expert attribution. See our Local AI Registry case study for how we pitched an AI visibility guarantee.

How much does a beauty PR agency cost in New York?

Most of our clients work on a monthly retainer, and we also take on project work for single launches, events and award seasons. We quote after a short discovery call so the plan matches your launch calendar and goals.
