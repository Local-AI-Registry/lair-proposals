About us, answered

Who is Colleen Mathis?

Colleen Mathis is the founder and CEO of absolute R relations, a New York City PR agency she started in 2015. She has more than 20 years of PR and marketing experience across beauty, lifestyle and luxury, including roles at large and boutique agencies and a PR Director role at a beauty-focused agency.

What does the R in absolute R relations stand for?

Results. The agency's tagline is The R is for Results, and every plan is measured by placements, creator content and awards that help brands sell.

Who is on the absolute R relations team?

Colleen Mathis leads every account as Founder and CEO. Erin Daly, Account Executive, leads influencer campaigns and event production, with placements in Fashionista, Good Housekeeping, Forbes, Allure and Byrdie. Absolute R Productions provides photo and video.
