What America searches before it books

Nineteen ready-to-pitch stories from Google search data: beauty gift guides, beauty timing, tattoos, nails, weed and liquor, and which TV shows, hobbies, drinks and Thanksgiving dishes go with Botox, state by state. Each one has a consumer angle, an editor angle and the exact numbers behind it.

How the numbers workGoogle scores each term 0 to 100. 100 is that term's busiest week in five years. On the daily charts, 100 is the busiest day in that window.
Compare timing, not sizeEach term was pulled on its own, so a 90 for Botox and a 90 for facelift are not the same number of people.

The beauty gift calendar: gadgets on Black Friday, perfume in December, red light after Christmas

Two holiday seasons in a row, beauty gift searches peak in three waves. Silk pillowcases and hair dryer brushes hit their top week on Black Friday week. Perfume samplers build to mid-December, the last weeks to ship. Red light therapy and gua sha peak the week after Christmas, likely people looking up how to use what they just unwrapped.

Light therapy was last year's breakout beauty gift

On Black Friday week 2025, LED face mask searches were 2.4 times the same week in 2024. Red light therapy searches roughly doubled (72 vs 37), and they hit a new high the week after Christmas. Silk pillowcases and hair dryer brushes, by comparison, stayed about flat year over year.

Spa gift cards are the Christmas Eve gift

Spa gift card searches peak on Christmas Eve or Christmas Day, two years running, at about 4 times early-December levels. They jump 50% to 75% in the last two days before Christmas and stay high on Christmas Day itself. Massage gift cards peak on Christmas Eve too. Perfume gift sets, by contrast, run high all month starting Black Friday. In five years of weekly data, the week before Christmas is always the top week for spa gift cards.

Daily search interest, US, Black Friday to Dec 31, 2024. All three terms pulled together.

Readers: the last-minute gift that doesn't need shipping. Editors: a ready angle for last-minute gift guides, with local spas and med spas as the answer.

Note: Botox and med spa gift card searches are too small to chart on their own.

Friday the 13th is tattoo day

On each of the last six Friday the 13ths, “tattoo near me” searches ran 2.2 to 3.2 times a normal Friday. Yesterday, Nov 13, 2026, came in at 3.0 times. “Piercing near me” rose about 1.7 times. Botox and hair salon searches stayed flat.

Daily search interest, US, Oct 16 to Nov 13, 2026. All three terms pulled together.

Readers: the shop tradition of Friday the 13th flash tattoos, and how to pick a shop when everyone is booking. Editors: six for six since September 2024, with numbers from yesterday.

Note: Nov 13 is the newest day in Google's data and can still shift a little.

Real patternPitch: this week

Thanksgiving, not New Year's Eve, is liquor store day. It rivals 4/20 for weed.

Thanksgiving Day was the top day for “liquor store near me” from mid-November to April, two years running: 2.4 times New Year's Eve, and ahead of Christmas Day. Dispensary searches on Thanksgiving and Christmas matched or beat 4/20 both years (this past season 37, 37 and 26; the season before 32, 33 and 32). In four years of weekly data, Thanksgiving week and Christmas week lifted dispensary searches more than 4/20 week every time.

Daily search interest, US, Nov 15, 2025 to Apr 30, 2026. Both terms pulled together.

Readers: the likely reason is “who's open today?” Many stores close or cut hours on the holiday, so people check before they drive. Editors: weed's biggest search days aren't 4/20, and Thanksgiving is Nov 26.

Note: industry coverage still calls 4/20 the biggest sales day, so pitch this as searches, not sales. 4/20 fell on a Monday in 2026 and on Easter Sunday in 2025.

Nail Salon Wednesday, Stretchy Pants Thursday

The day before Thanksgiving, “nail salon near me” searches run 1.7 to 1.9 times a normal Wednesday, about as busy as a Saturday (2024 and 2025). On Thanksgiving Day itself, “stretchy pants” searches jump 6 to 10 times a normal day. Tummy tuck searches don't budge.

Daily search interest, US, Nov 11 to Dec 8, 2024. All three terms pulled together.

Readers: book your nails before Wednesday. The stretchy pants are a given. Editors: a two-day Thanksgiving beauty calendar with a punchline.

Check before pitchingPitch: late December

January hits the liquor store harder than the dispensary

In each of the last five Januaries, liquor store searches sat 13% to 22% below their October and early November level. Dispensary searches held up better every year, anywhere from 20% down to 4% up.

Weekly search interest, US, Sep 2025 to Mar 2026. Each line is scaled to its own five-year peak.

Readers: Dry January, or “California sober”? Editors: a Dry January data point that isn't a survey.

Hold: more states legalized over these five years, which lifts dispensary searches over time and can flatter January. The 2022 gap was small (20% vs 22% down).

Tested and dropped this round: No Shave November vs barber and waxing searches, hangover vs facials and Botox, sunburn vs chemical peels, margarita vs spray tan, Valentine's Day nail salon searches, and state maps comparing weed, liquor, Ozempic, Botox and lip filler searches. None showed a pattern strong enough to pitch.
