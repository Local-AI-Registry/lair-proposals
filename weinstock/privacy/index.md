# Privacy Notice

The Weinstock Laser Eye Center respects your privacy and protects your health information as required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

## Information we collect on this website

When you request a consultation, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email and the procedure you are interested in. We use this information only to contact you about your care. We do not sell your information.

## Your health information

Our Notice of Privacy Practices explains how we may use and share your medical information, and your rights to see, copy and correct your records, to request limits on how we share them, and to receive a list of certain disclosures. Please do not send detailed medical information through the website form.

## Cookies and analytics

This website uses basic analytics to understand which pages are helpful. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings.
