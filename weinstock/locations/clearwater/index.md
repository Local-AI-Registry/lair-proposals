## Clearwater office FAQ

Where is Dr. Weinstock's Clearwater office?

Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm. The office is closed Saturday and Sunday.

How do I book a consultation?

Call (727) 581-8706 or [request a consultation online](https://proposals.localairegistry.com/weinstock/#contact), and the team will call you back to schedule.

Does Dr. Weinstock see patients at more than one office?

Yes. Dr. Weinstock sees patients in Largo and Clearwater. See the [Largo office](https://proposals.localairegistry.com/weinstock/locations/largo/).

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of your medications, and your current glasses or contact lenses. Plan on a longer first visit, since your eyes may be dilated, and bring a driver.
