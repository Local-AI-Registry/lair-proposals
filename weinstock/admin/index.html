What's locked, and why

Some parts of the site are locked so they always match Google, ChatGPT and medical directories. That is how the practice gets found and trusted. Your Local AI Registry team manages these for you:

  • Search data behind the scenes (schema), page titles and descriptions
  • Main headings, page addresses, menus and links between pages
  • Practice name, address, phone, hours, license and credentials
  • Procedure pages, location pages and medical articles

Ask for any of these anyway, and the assistant will send the request to your team.

Changes go live in about a minute. The assistant can make mistakes, so check the page after each change.
