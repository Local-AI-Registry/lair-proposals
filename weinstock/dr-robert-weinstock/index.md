About Dr. Weinstock

## A surgeon who helps shape _the field he practices in_

Robert J. Weinstock, MD, is a board-certified ophthalmologist who specializes in cataract and refractive surgery, including laser cataract surgery, premium and light adjustable lens implants, LASIK, EVO ICL and refractive lens exchange. He practices at The Eye Institute of West Florida in Largo and Clearwater, where he is Medical Director, and serves as Regional Medical Director for EyeCare Partners.

He trained in cataract and refractive surgery under his father, Stephen M. Weinstock, MD, completing his fellowship at The Eye Institute of West Florida in 2002. Since then he has served as principal investigator on 32 clinical studies of new lenses, lasers and surgical systems, written 14 textbook chapters, and lectured at major meetings including ASCRS, the American Academy of Ophthalmology and AECOS.

He has written textbook chapters on microincisional and 3D heads-up digital cataract surgery, helped develop TrueVision 3D surgical visualization, and teaches the next generation as Fellowship Director at The Eye Institute of West Florida and Ophthalmology Rotation Director for Evara Health residents.

He has written more than 200 published articles and given more than 500 lectures internationally. Outside the operating room, he lives in Tampa Bay with his wife and three sons, and spends his free time fishing, freediving, spearfishing and snowboarding.
