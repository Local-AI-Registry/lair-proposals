About Dr. Weinstock

A surgeon who helps shape the field he practices in

Robert J. Weinstock, MD, is a board-certified ophthalmologist who specializes in cataract and refractive surgery, including laser cataract surgery, premium and light adjustable lens implants, LASIK, EVO ICL and refractive lens exchange. He practices at The Eye Institute of West Florida in Largo, with offices in Clearwater, St. Petersburg and Tampa.

He trained in cataract and refractive surgery under his father, Stephen M. Weinstock, MD, completing his fellowship at The Eye Institute of West Florida in 2002. Since then he has served as principal investigator on 32 clinical studies of new lenses, lasers and surgical systems, written 14 textbook chapters, and lectured at major meetings including ASCRS, the American Academy of Ophthalmology and AECOS.

He has written textbook chapters on microincisional and 3D heads-up digital cataract surgery, chairs the TrueVision Systems advisory board, and teaches the next generation as an Assistant Clinical Professor at the University of South Florida.

Questions

About Dr. Weinstock

Is Dr. Robert Weinstock board certified?

Yes. Dr. Weinstock has been a Diplomate of the American Board of Ophthalmology since 2003. He also holds American Board of Eye Surgery sub-specialty certifications in refractive surgery (LASIK) and cataract and implant surgery, both earned in 2007.

Where did Dr. Weinstock train?

He earned his MD at The Chicago Medical School, completed his ophthalmology residency at Saint Louis University School of Medicine, where he served as Clinical and Administrative Chief Resident, and completed a fellowship in cataract and refractive surgery at The Eye Institute of West Florida.

Where does Dr. Weinstock see patients?

Dr. Weinstock sees patients at The Eye Institute of West Florida in Largo, with additional offices in Clearwater, St. Petersburg and Tampa. He performs surgery at the Largo Ambulatory Surgery Center, where he is Medical Director.

What procedures does Dr. Weinstock specialize in?

He specializes in cataract and refractive surgery, including laser cataract surgery, premium and light adjustable lens implants, LASIK, EVO ICL and refractive lens exchange.

Is Dr. Weinstock involved in research and teaching?

Yes. He has served on 42 clinical studies, 32 of them as principal investigator, written 14 textbook chapters and is an Assistant Clinical Professor of Ophthalmology at the University of South Florida Eye Institute.

What awards has Dr. Weinstock received?

His honors include Newsweek's America's Best Eye Doctors (2022), Ocular Surgery News Premier Surgeon 300 (2016), Castle Connolly Top Doctor (2017), the American Academy of Ophthalmology Achievement Award (2009) and Tampa Bay Metro Magazine Top Doctors in ten editions from 2013 to 2026.
