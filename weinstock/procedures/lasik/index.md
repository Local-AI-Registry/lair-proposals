LASIK is a laser procedure that reshapes the cornea, the clear front surface of the eye, so light focuses correctly without glasses or contacts. It is FDA approved to treat nearsightedness, farsightedness and astigmatism in adults with a stable prescription and healthy corneas. Most people notice sharper vision within a day.

**Who it suits** Adults with a stable prescription and healthy corneas

**Treats** Nearsightedness, farsightedness, astigmatism

**Recovery** Most see clearly within a day

**Not for** Age-related reading vision changes

## How LASIK works

A laser creates a thin flap in the cornea, a second laser reshapes the tissue beneath it, and the flap is laid back in place. The treatment takes minutes per eye, and you go home the same day.

## Who is a good candidate

Good candidates are adults whose prescription has been stable, with corneas that are healthy and thick enough to treat. A thorough evaluation checks your prescription, corneal thickness and shape, and screens for dry eye, which LASIK can make worse.

If LASIK is not the best fit, an implantable lens such as [EVO ICL](https://proposals.localairegistry.com/weinstock/procedures/evo-icl/) may be. Compare them in [LASIK vs. EVO ICL: how to know which fits you](https://proposals.localairegistry.com/weinstock/articles/lasik-vs-evo-icl/).

## What to expect

  1. **Evaluation.** Detailed corneal mapping and measurements decide if LASIK is safe for you.
  2. **Treatment.** Numbing drops and a few minutes of laser time per eye.
  3. **Recovery.** Rest the day of surgery, then a checkup within the first 24 to 48 hours.

## Over 45?

LASIK does not stop presbyopia, the age-related loss of reading vision that usually starts in the mid-40s. For many people at that stage, [Clear Lens Exchange](https://proposals.localairegistry.com/weinstock/procedures/clear-lens-exchange/) may be a better fit.

## LASIK FAQ

Is LASIK permanent?

LASIK permanently reshapes the cornea, but the eye can still change with age, including the need for reading glasses after about 45.

Does LASIK hurt?

Numbing drops are used, and most people feel pressure rather than pain. Some mild irritation is common for a few hours afterward.

Can LASIK cause dry eye?

Yes. LASIK can cause or worsen dry eye, which is why you are screened for it before treatment.

How do I know if I qualify?

A full evaluation of your prescription, corneal thickness and shape, and eye health determines whether LASIK is safe and effective for you.

What if I am not a LASIK candidate?

Other options include EVO ICL, an implantable lens that does not remove corneal tissue, and Clear Lens Exchange for people over about 45.
