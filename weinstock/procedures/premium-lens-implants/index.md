Premium lens implants are advanced intraocular lenses placed during cataract surgery or lens replacement that do more than restore clear vision. Toric lenses correct astigmatism, multifocal and extended-depth-of-focus lenses help with near and distance vision, and the Light Adjustable Lens can be fine-tuned after surgery. They are for people who want to rely less on glasses after surgery.

**Who it suits** Cataract or lens replacement patients who want fewer glasses

**Options** Toric, multifocal, extended depth of focus, Light Adjustable Lens

**Recovery** Same as cataract surgery

**Coverage** Insurance covers the standard lens; the upgrade is extra

## Your lens options

### Toric lenses

Correct astigmatism, so distance vision can be sharp without glasses.

### Multifocal and extended-depth-of-focus lenses

Provide a range of focus for distance, computer and reading vision, reducing the need for readers.

### Light Adjustable Lens

A lens whose power can be adjusted after surgery with a series of light treatments in the office, so your final vision can be fine-tuned. Dr. Weinstock chose this lens for his own eyes.

## How to choose

The right lens depends on your eye measurements, your eye health and how you spend your day. Someone who reads and drives at night has different needs than a golfer or a pilot. ## Premium Lens Implants FAQ

Which premium lens is best?

There is no single best lens. The right choice depends on your measurements, eye health and lifestyle, which your consultation covers in detail.

Will a premium lens get rid of my glasses completely?

Many patients rarely need glasses after a premium lens, but some still use readers for small print or glasses for night driving. Your surgeon will set honest expectations.

Can I get a premium lens with Medicare?

Yes. Medicare covers the standard lens and surgery, and you pay the difference for the premium portion.

What is the Light Adjustable Lens?

It is an intraocular lens whose power can be adjusted after surgery with light treatments, so your vision can be fine-tuned once your eye has healed.

Do premium lenses cause halos?

Some multifocal lenses can cause halos or glare around lights at night, especially early on. This is one of the trade-offs discussed when choosing a lens.
