Clear Lens Exchange, also called refractive lens exchange, replaces the eye's natural lens with an artificial lens before a cataract forms, to correct your prescription and reduce the need for glasses. It is usually for adults over about 45 who need help with both distance and reading vision, or who are not good candidates for LASIK. Because the natural lens is replaced, a cataract can never develop.

**Who it suits** Adults over about 45, or high prescriptions

**Treats** Distance, reading vision and astigmatism

**Bonus** You will never develop a cataract

**Recovery** Similar to cataract surgery

## How Clear Lens Exchange works

The procedure is the same as modern cataract surgery: the natural lens is removed through a tiny opening and replaced with an intraocular lens chosen for your eyes. With a [premium lens](https://proposals.localairegistry.com/weinstock/procedures/premium-lens-implants/), many people reduce or eliminate their need for glasses for both near and far.

## Who is a good candidate

Clear Lens Exchange often fits people in their mid-40s and older who are starting to need reading glasses, people with high prescriptions, and people who are not candidates for [LASIK](https://proposals.localairegistry.com/weinstock/procedures/lasik/). ## Clear Lens Exchange FAQ

How is Clear Lens Exchange different from cataract surgery?

The procedure is the same. The difference is timing: Clear Lens Exchange replaces a lens that is still clear, to correct vision rather than to remove a cataract.

Will I need reading glasses after?

With a multifocal, extended-depth-of-focus or Light Adjustable Lens, many people need readers rarely or not at all. Your consultation sets realistic expectations.

Is insurance going to cover it?

Usually not, because it is an elective procedure. You receive a written quote first.

Can I have it if I already had LASIK?

Often yes. Your earlier LASIK is factored into the lens calculations.

How long does the new lens last?

An intraocular lens is designed to last for the rest of your life.
