Questions

Answers before you visit

How do I know if I need cataract surgery?

Common signs include cloudy or dim vision, glare and halos around lights, faded colors and trouble driving at night. A comprehensive eye exam with Dr. Weinstock is the only way to know for sure.

Am I a candidate for LASIK?

Good LASIK candidates are adults with a stable prescription and healthy corneas. If your corneas are thin or your prescription is high, EVO ICL or Clear Lens Exchange may be a better fit. Your consultation will tell you which.

Does Medicare cover cataract surgery?

Medicare and most insurance plans typically cover medically necessary cataract surgery. Our team reviews your coverage, and any cost for premium lens options, before your procedure.

Where is Weinstock Vision located?

Our main office is at 1225 West Bay Drive in Largo, Florida, and we serve patients from Clearwater, St. Petersburg and Tampa. Surgery is performed at the Largo Ambulatory Surgery Center, where Dr. Weinstock is Medical Director.

What are your office hours?

We are open Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, and closed Saturday and Sunday.

Who will perform my surgery?
